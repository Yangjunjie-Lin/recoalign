# Retired Secondary Measurement

PIVOT_EXP_A3 v1 failed its complete-field free-response contract during development before any
held-out inference. PIVOT_EXP_A3R prospectively narrowed the response surface to a continuation
after `FINAL_CHOICE=`, but the complete 160-trial development smoke produced only 127 valid
continuations (parse rate 0.79375). The 33 invalid continuations were entity identifiers rather than
option identifiers: E2 × 25, E4 × 6, and E1 × 2. Every raw output remains preserved in the A3R
development smoke artifact.

The instrument was retired before validation. No validation outcome was observed, no primary
outcome was selected before retirement, and no formal adjudication occurred. The evidence does not
support a claim about open-ended generation ability or semantic failure. No third prompt repair was
attempted, and no restricted-generation substitute or entity-to-option mapping is used.

The independent PIVOT_EXP_A3P study retains this negative evidence and evaluates only the frozen
27,000-row primary conditional-likelihood instrument.
