# EXP004 Failure Taxonomy Report

Stage-3 failures are retained as `object_failure`, `relation_failure`, `multi_hop_failure`, or
`structure_misuse`. Counts are descriptive and do not change predictions, accuracy, or decisions.
Per-seed prediction rows and aggregate counts are stored in `outputs/EXP004/`.

The taxonomy is intended for later mechanism analysis. It does not justify a Type-A, Type-B, or
Type-C claim by itself; classification requires the preregistered SAS/StAS/RES measurements and an
eligible frozen model.
