# Mechanistic evidence report

The mechanistic analysis implementation is complete. The controlled toy suite
records paired no-structure, random-token, parameter-matched, probe, and
remove/shuffle/replace intervention outputs for seeds 101/202/303.

These outputs validate the instrumentation and causal tensor contract only. They are
not evidence that ReCoAlign improves a real VLM. The real-model gate remains blocked
until eligible EXP004 diagnosis, trained hidden-context checkpoints, and complete
multi-VLM benchmark cells exist.
