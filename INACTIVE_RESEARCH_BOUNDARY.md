# Inactive research boundary

These modules are retained for provenance but are not scientifically authorized.

The boundary covers:

- `training/`
- `models/reasoning_interface/`
- `models/alignment/`
- `configs/training/`
- `configs/benchmarks/comprehensive_matrix.yaml`
- `configs/ablations/`

Historical code and configs may be inspected and tested for reproducibility. They are not pending
work and must not be used to train ReCoAlign, start the comprehensive matrix, design an adapter,
structure token, loss, or method, or support a claim-bearing manuscript. Reactivation requires a
new independent governance decision outside the closed ReCoAlign program.
