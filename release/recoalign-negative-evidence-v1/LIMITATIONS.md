# Limitations

- Scientific inference is restricted to one frozen LLaVA-1.5-7B checkpoint revision.
- EXP003 failed its preregistered token-length integrity gate and provides no OOD conclusion.
- EXP004 rejects the required high-SAS premise but does not test every hidden layer or every notion
  of semantic content.
- PIVOT_EXP_A supported multiple explanations and did not identify a dominant cause.
- PIVOT_EXP_A2 failed semantic-sufficiency and parsing-integrity gates.
- PIVOT_EXP_A3 and PIVOT_EXP_A3R are failed measurement-instrument records, not semantic evidence.
- PIVOT_EXP_A3P validates a primary scoring instrument but neither semantic rescue candidate.
- The resource does not contain third-party checkpoint bytes or restricted benchmark data, so
  integrity-only reproduction is distinct from rerunning inference.
- No independent backbone replication is authorized or represented.
- The technical report is a closeout record, not peer-reviewed research publication.
