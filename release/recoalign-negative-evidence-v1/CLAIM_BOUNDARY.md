# Claim Boundary

## Supported, scope-bounded statement

In the frozen LLaVA-1.5-7B execution at revision
`4481d270cc22fd5c4d1bb5df129622006ccd9234`, correct supplied relational evidence outperformed
partial, corrupt, or random supplied evidence in EXP002. This is evidence sensitivity within one
frozen backbone and protocol.

## Falsified or unsupported statements

- A graph is a privileged reasoning interface over information-equivalent captions.
- ReCoAlign is a validated or effective method.
- A Structured Reasoning Interface Gap exists.
- Visual semantic content is sufficiently available for the proposed method line.
- All hidden layers lack visual semantics.
- Semantic rescue by M1 or M2 succeeded.
- PIVOT_EXP_A isolated a causally dominant mechanism.
- The findings establish a general vision-language-model mechanism.

## Inconclusive statements

EXP003 cannot support an OOD claim because its preregistered token-length integrity gate failed.
PIVOT_EXP_A2 cannot support semantic sufficiency or causal separation because semantic-sufficiency
and parsing-integrity gates failed.

## Measurement boundary

PIVOT_EXP_A3P established validity of its primary conditional-likelihood prediction inventory and
scoring instrument. That measurement-validity result is not evidence of semantic validity,
semantic comprehension, or a successful manipulation.

## Publication boundary

article, is not peer reviewed, and is not submission ready.
