# Instrument Retirement Rationale

PIVOT_EXP_A3 v1 failed its full-field free-response contract before held-out inference.
PIVOT_EXP_A3R then prospectively narrowed that external-validity instrument to a continuation
contract ending in `FINAL_CHOICE=`. Its complete development smoke produced 127 valid continuations
from 160 trials (0.79375), with 33 invalid entity identifiers: E2 25 times, E4 6 times, and E1 twice.
All trials were deterministic and the CUDA/NF4 execution stack was healthy.

The failure therefore invalidates the response instrument; it does not establish model or semantic
failure. A third surface repair would create additional researcher degrees of freedom after two
development failures. The free-response instrument is permanently retired before validation.
No held-out validation prediction, scientific metric, or formal candidate decision was observed.

PIVOT_EXP_A3P is an independent primary-only study. It inherits the 27,000 frozen forced-choice
trials exactly and uses no response interpretation, alias mapping, answer reconstruction, fallback,
or restricted-generation substitute.
