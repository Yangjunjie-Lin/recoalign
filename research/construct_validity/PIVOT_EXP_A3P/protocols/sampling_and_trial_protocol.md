# Sampling and Trial Protocol

Validation is the forced-choice subset of the frozen PIVOT_EXP_A3 inventory: 27,000 rows, five
registered seeds, 100 scenes per seed, and 54 cells per scene. The 27,000 parent trial keys are
retained exactly. Administrative `study_id` is the only row-level change.

The eight development scenes use seed 20260830 and are disjoint from validation seeds and from the
PIVOT_EXP_A2 seeds. Development contains 432 forced-choice cells and cannot contribute correctness
or scientific contrasts. No seed, scene, image, trial, or failed measurement may be deleted.
