# PIVOT_EXP_A3P Research Question

Can the frozen forced-choice conditional-likelihood measurement establish that the M1 or M2
semantic manipulation is sufficient, discriminable, and non-interfering under images without any
free-text response or answer interpretation layer?

PIVOT_EXP_A3P can validate answer-option mapping comprehension, semantic-scaffold comprehension,
scene-truth semantic sufficiency, oracle/corrupted intervention separation, and image interference.

It cannot validate open-ended answer generation, instruction-following robustness, natural-language
response formatting, a semantic-primary mechanism, an integration-independent mechanism, a joint
causal mechanism, ReCoAlign method effectiveness, or any paper-level claim.
