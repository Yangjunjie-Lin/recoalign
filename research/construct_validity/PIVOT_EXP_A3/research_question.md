# PIVOT_EXP_A3 Research Question

## Study title

Semantic Manipulation and Answer Contract Construct Validation

## Scope

PIVOT_EXP_A3 asks whether a frozen LLaVA-1.5-7B measurement can separately establish:

1. an answer contract that always yields a registered four-way choice without mixing generation
   grammar with semantic correctness; and
2. a relation-free, answer-free semantic manipulation that makes object shape, color, identity,
   and entity–attribute binding sufficiently available.

The diagnostic decomposition is prospective and construct-specific:

- **CV1 — Answer Contract Comprehension:** Can the model map four displayed alternatives to numeric
  option IDs and use the frozen primary/secondary contracts?
- **CV2 — Semantic Scaffold Comprehension:** Can the model read entity IDs, shapes, colors, and
  complete bindings from the active scaffold when the scene image is replaced by one fixed neutral
  image?
- **CV3 — Semantic Rescue Against Scene Truth:** With the original scene present, does the oracle
  scaffold make each primitive sufficiently available, and is it separated from a fully false
  binding corruption?

PIVOT_EXP_A3 does not test a semantic-primary, integration-independent, joint-causal, or model
mechanism claim. It cannot reinterpret or rescore PIVOT_EXP_A2. A successful outcome authorizes at
most a separately preregistered PIVOT_EXP_A4 causal-separation study on the same frozen backbone.
