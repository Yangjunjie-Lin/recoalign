# Mechanistic Analysis Protocol

The mechanism claim is accepted only when the ordering SAS > StAS and a positive oracle-graph gain
is replicated across eligible frozen VLMs. A high SAS with lower StAS is classified as a Type-B
structure-interface failure. High SAS and StAS with low RES is Type-C reasoning-execution failure.
Low SAS is Type-A semantic failure. Missing hidden states, unpinned adapters, incomplete seeds, and
ReferenceVLM-only results remain INCONCLUSIVE.

The interface-gap score is SAS minus StAS. RES is oracle-graph accuracy, while oracle gain is
oracle-graph accuracy minus image-only accuracy. Corrupted-graph sensitivity is reported separately
to show that the oracle improvement is structure-dependent.
